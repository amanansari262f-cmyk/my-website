About

How the assistant works

AI Health Assistant reads your symptoms in plain English or Hinglish, matches them against a curated knowledge base of {{ total_conditions }} common conditions, and returns easy-to-follow self-care steps, warning signs and a short video guide.

What makes it smart

  • Multi-symptom matching — “fever, cough and sir dard” returns ranked results instead of just the first hit.
  • Typo & Hinglish friendly — understands words like bukhar, khansi, pet dard and forgives spelling mistakes.
  • Negation aware — “no fever, only cough” ignores the fever.
  • Emergency detection — phrases such as chest pain or trouble breathing trigger an urgent banner.
  • Voice input, history & wellness tools — speak your symptoms, revisit past checks and calculate BMI or daily water needs.

Important

This project is for education and awareness only. It is not a medical device and does not provide a diagnosis. Always consult a qualified doctor for medical concerns.
